Inspiration

Combo

In Devil May Cry, released in 2001, a combo system was invented that increases based on the player's movements and attacks, as well as whether they take damage. ULTRAKILL adopts this combo system. Depending on the weapons used, the player's weapon diversification, and whether they take damage, their scores will increase or decrease.

Combat style

Inspired by DOOM 64
Ultrakill also features fast-paced gameplay and first-person shooter elements
Also known as Fast-FPS

Dynamism of the fight

Popularized by Devil May Cry (2001)
Ultrakill was inspired by its combo system
which progresses from D to SSS as your fighting style improves

The lore

The game's lore is inspired by a collection of poems called the Divine Comedy. It recounts the main character's journey through Hell.
In the game, our character explores different floors of Hell, each with its own distinct setting.
